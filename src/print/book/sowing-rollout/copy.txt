[sweet_corn]
@Sow indoors | Mid-Apr in cool districts. | 4 |
@Sow | Outdoors from mid-May in warm soil. Later than mid-Jun, only quick varieties or baby corn. | 5 | 6
@Plant out | Late May–Jun after hardening off. | 5-6 |
Sow | Place one or two seeds in each pot or spot. Keep the stronger seedling. Use pots for an early start or sow in the final bed when the soil is warm.
Plant out | Harden off and plant with the rootball intact. Water in. For full-sized cobs, make a block of at least four short rows so pollen reaches the silks. Baby corn can grow in rows because the cobs are picked before pollination.
%harvest|1|As silks brown, test a kernel. Milky juice means the cob is ready, clear juice is too early and doughy kernels are past their best. Check often.
~Frit fly|sign|Ragged leaves on stunted seedlings.
~Frit fly|act|Sow into warm soil to keep seedlings growing.
~Cross pollination|act|Isolate corn types as the seed packet advises.

[tomato_greenhouse]
@Sow indoors | Late Feb–Mar. Late Jan only with heat and good light. | 2-3 | 1
@Plant under glass | Apr–May once warm. | 4-5 |
Sow indoors | Sow thinly in trays or small pots of seed compost at about {64°F~18°C}. Keep moist and give emerging seedlings plenty of light.
Prick out and pot on | Move tray seedlings into pots at their first pair of true leaves. Support the roots and hold a leaf. Pot on as the roots fill the pot.
Plant in the greenhouse | Wait until nights stay around {50°F~10°C} or warmer. Water well-rooted plants and set them slightly deeper in pots, growbags or an enriched bed. Stand cordon pots {24 in.~60 cm} apart centre to centre. Support plants with canes or string.
+Containers | One per {12–18 in.~30–45 cm} pot, two per standard growbag. | planting
+Bed spacing | {18–24 in.~45–60 cm}, wider for vigorous cordons. | plant_spacing
^Grow cordon tomatoes on a single stem for fruit from summer into autumn. Give them bright light, steady moisture and good ventilation. Pots and growbags dry out quickly.
%care|2|Water evenly and feed organically at the product rate as fruit develops. Ventilate in heat and gently tap flowering trusses. Reduce water as autumn growth slows.
~Aphids|act|Squash damaging colonies and encourage predators.
