Ridge cucumbers need a warm, sheltered bed. Choose an outdoor variety for slicing or gherkins for pickling.
Train stems up a trellis if space is short.

Recommended varieties
STANDARD RIDGE
Marketmore 76
A well-established ridge cucumber for outdoor
growing in a warm, sheltered position. Produces dark￾green slicing fruits.
STANDARD RIDGE
Bush Champion
A compact outdoor cucumber suited to smaller
gardens and large containers. A useful alternative to
vigorous trailing selections.
JAPANESE
Burpless Tasty Green
A long-fruited outdoor cucumber for a sheltered
summer bed. Provide support where practical and pick
fruits regularly.
APPLE
Crystal Lemon
An unusual cucumber with round, pale-yellow fruits.
Pick young for the best texture; a novelty alongside a
standard slicing variety.
Key risks
Mildew
Powdery mildew often
appears on older leaves
later in the season.
Slugs and snails
Slugs and snails may chew
seedlings and low fruit.
Lack of warmth
If sowing or planting
conditions are cold, plants
grow slowly and become
easy prey for slugs,
woodlice and damping-off
diseases.
Cucumber mosaic
virus
Cucumber mosaic virus can
cause mottled, puckered
leaves, stunting and
distorted fruit.
