# Image-led cover concepts

11 November 2026. John requested complete front-cover generation using T05 and Anna Greenland’s *Grow Easy* (Mitchell Beazley) as direct seeds. He found the first image insufficiently adventurous and specifically requested text spilling across areas.

**Selected background: revision 16 midpoint yellow.** John accepted it with ‘That’s great.’ The [colour decision](COLOUR-SELECTION.json) binds the choice to its exact image and preserves the earlier sage decision. White title/sticker and dark-green supporting type carry forward. Earlier sage and yellow variants remain comparisons; this is not completed-cover or production approval.

**Selected typeface: Lilita One, following full-cover trial 08H.** John chose it for its relationship to the interior and asked whether a slightly slimmer version exists. The [type decision and availability check](TYPE-SELECTION.json) records the selected image and actual local font. Google Fonts’ official family metadata lists only the single Regular/400 face. No lighter or condensed version is supplied. John then authorised a modest size-and-spacing trial, retaining the natural font weight.

**Accepted lettering: revision 09.** John said, ‘Ok let’s go with that.’ Its modestly smaller, more openly spaced Lilita title is the accepted visual type treatment; the prompt’s numerical size/spacing targets are not measured font metrics.

**Selected photograph: 12A, tending tomatoes.** John chose the person-led direction in 11A and confirmed that its likeness was good, then requested less staged gardening action. Revision 12 supplied A tending tomatoes, B weeding and C pushing a wheelbarrow. John said, ‘Great - let’s use A - it looks very natural.’ The [revision 12 record](ARTWORK-12.json) binds that selection to the exact image. B/C and the earlier harvest-table trials remain comparisons. These are generated scenes, not documentary photographs of the actual garden.

**Saved candidate: revision 17, photographic direction.** On 12 November John requested that this cover and its associated files be documented and saved, then agreed to explore the new illustrated route before choosing between the original three directions. The [saved candidate](../cover-comparisons/saved-photographic-2026-11-12/README.md) preserves the current cover and complete working evidence. The [comparison tracker](../cover-comparisons/README.md) owns the active route. The YouTube feature is retained in this candidate; saving it is not final-cover or supplier approval.

The preceding [font list](font-list/Title-font-comparison.png) and three full-cover studies remain preserved. 08A Charter and 08E Avenir are comparisons, not the active type direction.

The selected midpoint yellow, centred rectangular photo and overlapping white circular sticker remain visually consistent. Title scale/spacing adapts to the natural font shapes; the old curled serif G tail is intentionally removed. These are generated interpretations guided by real-font references, not font-embedded production files. The one-typeface-per-cover constraint remains fixed. The [font-list source record](font-list/FONTS.json), [renderer](font-list/build-font-list.py), earlier 07B and all original artwork remain preserved.
