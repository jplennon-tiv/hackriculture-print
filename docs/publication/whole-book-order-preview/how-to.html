Use the calendar and scales to compare crops and plan their care.

Reading the calendar

Bright shades show the usual sowing and harvest months for temperate climates: here, lowland England (US zones 8 to 9). Muted shades show less usual options for warmer climates. Use local conditions and the crop’s advice to choose your timing.

Difficulty rating

Difficulty reflects ease of growing and tolerance of adverse conditions. Radishes suit beginners; celery and cauliflower can defeat even experienced gardeners in a bad season.

Core needs

Too little sun, water or manure can damage some plants, while too much can harm others. Use the scales as a guide to each crop’s needs.
