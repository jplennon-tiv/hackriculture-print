Grow onions and shallots for fresh bulbs, salads, pickles and winter stores. Spring sets offer a simple start; seed
gives more choice of colour, size and season. Shallots usually form clusters; overwintered onions crop earlier. All
need clean, fertile ground and thorough drying for storage. Timings and bolting risk vary.
