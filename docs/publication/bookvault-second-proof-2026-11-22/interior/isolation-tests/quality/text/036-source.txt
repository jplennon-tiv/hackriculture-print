A cauliflower needs steady growth to make a tight curd. Drought, crowding or a check at transplanting can leave
tiny heads. Choose the variety for your season and give it firm soil, water and room.

Recommended varieties
SUMMER
Clapton F1
A clubroot-resistant white
cauliflower; combine with good
rotation and soil care.
SUMMER
All The Year Round
White heads for successive
sowings; not an all-year outdoor
crop.
SUMMER
Graffiti
Purple heads add colour alongside
white varieties.
ROMANESCO
Veronica F1
Pointed lime-green Romanesco curds offer distinctive
shape and texture.
WINTER
Aalsmeer
An overwintering type for spring crops. Sow in early
summer; protect through winter.
