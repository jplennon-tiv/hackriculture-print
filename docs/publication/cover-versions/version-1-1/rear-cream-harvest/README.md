This first proposal for the back accompanies the selected [Original harvest front](../original-harvest-light-study/README.md). The original paper and grain layers reflect across the joining edge. The only vegetable group on the back is the archived [Kitchen roots upper arrangement with its cast shadows](../../../../assets/harvest-alternatives/with-cover-shadows/03-kitchen-roots-upper.png).

The [draft copy](copy.json) draws on the 44 growing guides, 14 troubleshooting guides, organic growing techniques and [John’s introduction](../../../introduction-revision/introduction.txt). The [layout](layout.swift) sets Lilita One and Nunito Sans as live text. The working price comes from the shared book copy. A 52 × 30 mm white field holds the ISBN/barcode position pending supply of the number. The second page places the two trim panels together for comparison. Supplier spine and bleed are a later stage.
