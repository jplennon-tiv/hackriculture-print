The crop symbols retain the existing brassica green; risks remain dark single-colour. All artwork and internal cutouts are transparent. Screen zoom affects physical size.

My preference: B has the closest relationship to the crop illustrations. C offers a stronger personality and merits a small-page test, particularly for risks. D is worth considering if a quieter, simpler set feels better. A remains a viable baseline. The two families can be paired deliberately—for example, B for growing advice and C for risks and bubbles.

Reference directions: RHS botanical art collection for plant observation and drawn character; Phosphor for the idea of consistent outline, fill and duotone families. These drawings are original, not copied or downloaded from either source. The woodcut interpretation and style recommendations are design judgements.
