# Parsnip one-off amendment

7 November 2026. [Review both editions](REVIEW.html). John approved both editions;
the [approval record](APPROVAL.json) binds the exact reviewed files.

John requested plain language in Tender and True's description. It now reads:
“A traditional parsnip with long roots and good flavour. Best grown in deep,
stone-free soil.” This deliberately replaces the editorial phrase “Keep as the
familiar open-pollinated alternative” with practical growing guidance. Traditional
status, long roots and flavour come from the existing short description; deep,
stone-free soil comes from this variety's full canonical description. It does
not introduce a new seed-saving recommendation. Other variety descriptions and
the canonical catalogue remain unchanged.

John requested a second Quick facts pass because the first arrangement looked
lopsided. The columns are now equal in width. Harvest, Germination, Sowing depth
and Yield form a two-by-two grid on the left, with Ready in spanning its full
width beneath. Sow, Row spacing and Plant spacing form three rows on the right.
The visible content heights are 30.46 mm and 29.16 mm in both units, a difference
of 1.30 mm. This supersedes the four-left/four-right trial. All eight facts,
exact values, qualifications, icons and type sizes remain.

John then asked whether the spare space could accommodate more varieties. A
second row now adds **White Gem**, **Hollow Crown Improved** and **The Student**
from the existing master catalogue. The original three entries remain in order.
The descriptions use only their canonical full text, condensed into practical
language; no master values, identities or ranks change. White Gem is ranked 6;
the other two are ranked 4. Avonresister, also ranked 4, remains in the master
but is not selected because a current seed supplier could not be verified. This
does not establish that it has been discontinued.

Supplier/name checks on 7 November:

- [Suttons lists White Gem](https://www.suttons.co.uk/vegetable-seeds/parsnip-seeds/parsnip-seeds-white-gem_MH-1333).
- [St. Clare lists the exact name Hollow Crown Improved](https://www.stclareseeds.com/product/parsnip-hollow-crown-improved/); this is a US supplier. The [RHS has a profile under that exact name](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/173862/pastinaca-sativa-hollow-crown-improved/details), marked unresolved. Its existing master identity is retained; no synonym claim is made for Hollow Crown or Improved Hollow Crowned.
- [Thomas Etty's 2026 catalogue](https://thomasetty.co.uk/seeds/vegetables-flowers-2026.pdf) and [Thompson & Morgan's heritage range](https://www.thompson-morgan.com/brands-heritage) list The Student. The catalogue's indexed text was readable, although direct PDF retrieval returned a server error. These support the name/listing only; the printed description remains sourced from the master.

The three selected descriptions and source paths are recorded in the amendment.
Six entries fit at the existing type size in two rows of three.

Soil & preparation loses the routine instruction “test acid soil before liming
to pH 6.5”; the sentence now ends “Rake clean.” The specific acid-soil risk
remains in the Canker diagnostic in Key risks. This preserves useful
crop-specific information while applying John's standing no-routine-liming rule.

Both actual PDFs remain two pages, 185 × 240 mm, numbered 72–73. Footer
clearances are 11.06 mm and 12.10 mm in both editions. The new variety row uses
19.39 mm of the opening page's previous spare space. All six varieties,
seven pest entries, original artwork, 9 pt planting text and See also links
remain. No pests are trimmed. Both PDF spreads were visually inspected against
the originals; the reader, links, images and unit selector were checked locally
and over the preview server. Original full readers, canonical data and the main
book remain unchanged.
