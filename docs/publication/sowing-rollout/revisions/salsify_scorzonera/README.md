# Salsify & Scorzonera one-off amendment

10 November 2026. [Review both editions](REVIEW.html). **Approved by John in both editions**, including the three-variety panel. The [approval record](APPROVAL.json) binds the reviewed files.

Looking after the crop now separates two practical jobs:

1. Thin salsify carefully to 4 in. / 10 cm apart. Follow the seed packet for scorzonera.
2. Keep young plants free of weeds. Hand-weed near the crowns; hoes can damage the narrow roots.

Each edition prints only its own unit. This removes repeated hand-weeding advice
and the doubled full stop in the original imperial thinning sentence. The other
two care points remain unchanged. The spacing, careful thinning, weed control
and root-damage warning are supported by the existing canonical care entries;
no research or new growing claims were needed. Canonical data is unchanged.

The standing checks regroup all eight Quick facts, giving Sow and Ready in the
full width of their half-page column. All values and icons remain. Soil &
preparation loses the general sentence “Aim for pH 6–7; test before liming.”
There is no definite crop-specific requirement here. Fine-seedbed and feeding
advice remain, without a replacement liming instruction.

Both editions retain two 185 × 240 mm pages, physical folios 82–83, three
varieties, all five pest rows and the existing artwork and type sizes. Footer clearances are 20.24 mm on the opening and 7.58 mm on the practical
page in both units. The Quick facts content heights are 38.33 mm and 43.09 mm.
No pest entries are omitted. This guide’s existing footer has no See also links.

## Additional variety, 10 November

At John’s request to use the spare opening-page space, add **Duplex (scorzonera)**:

> A productive scorzonera with long, dark-skinned roots and a delicate flavour.

The panel now uses three columns at the existing type sizes, retaining both
original descriptions. The change uses 8.47 mm of the previous footer clearance.
Page 2, all facts, artwork and pest entries remain as in the preceding amendment.
The finisher verified all three variety descriptions in both actual PDFs.

[Research and naming decisions](../../../../../../hackriculture-resources/summaries/salsify-scorzonera-extra-varieties/SOURCES.md)

because that name overlaps commercially with Duplex. No canonical aliases or
