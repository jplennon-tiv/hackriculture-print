# Gutter options for the final derivative

23 November 2026. Requested by John after endorsing the quality restorations. John selected Option 1: the first physical proof is comfortable. [Decision](QUALITY-DECISION.json). The gutter is the space between content and the binding. Measurements concern text, not decorative art intended to bleed.

Both tested gutter geometries have passed supplier validation with outlined lettering. The choice is therefore about the bound book, not upload acceptance.

| Option | Advantages | Trade-offs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restore original layout: nominal 17 mm inside / 8 mm outside; 160 mm body width | Preserves approved typography, wrapping, positioning and outer white space. Least additional work. Accepted with outlines. | Less breathing room at the binding; below the 20 mm guidance being used for this review. Whether that matters in use depends on how comfortably the physical book opens. |
| Keep the tested adjustment: measured minimum 20.1 mm inside / 5.2 mm outside; everything at 99% | More binding clearance; no text reflow or pagination change; already built, checked and accepted. | Gains space largely by moving the page outward. Outer edge is noticeably tighter, leaving less white space for fingers. All text and art is 1% smaller. It was a controlled workaround rather than a fresh layout design. |
| Properly lay out 20 mm inside / 8 mm outside: proposed 157 mm body width | More space at the binding while preserving the outer margin and intended type sizes. Best-balanced long-term option if 20 mm is wanted. | Body width reduces by 3 mm (about 1.9%); some lines may wrap differently. Every affected spread needs fit and visual checks. Aim to preserve 174 pages, but that must be verified before freezing the cover specification. |

Recommendation: option 3 is the quality-first choice for a final production layout, allowing another measured layout pass. Option 1 is a sensible faster choice if the existing physical proof is comfortable to read near the spine. Option 2 is useful as an accepted test/control, but is the least attractive finished-page compromise.

Original geometry is specified in [the design system](../../../../BOOK-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md). Measured native-lettering minimum on the original is 16.878 mm; the nominal design value is 17 mm. The [tested adjustment](../gutter-outline-retry/README.md) measures 20.109 mm inside / 5.182 mm outside. Neither measurement is a comprehensive OCR audit of raster labels. No layout change is installed by this options note.
