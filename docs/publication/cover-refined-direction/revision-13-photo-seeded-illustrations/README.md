# Photo-seeded house illustrations — Version 3

15 November 2026. John's requested experiment uses the two Version 2 photographs as composition references and restores the book's in-house drawing style for this version. The earlier front-strip style waiver does not apply to Version 3.

[Original Version 3 preview](../../cover-versions/version-3/references/pale-green-background/README.md) · [Layout and sources](LAYOUT.json) · [Exact prompts and reference roles](GENERATION.json) · [Visual and preservation checks](CHECKS.json).

The source crops are identical in framing to Version 2: P04 courgette flowers above, P01 tomato trusses below. Two separate generated illustrations retain their irregular stems, flower/fruit relationships and overlapping leaves. Approved courgette, greenhouse-tomato and capsicum heroes were supplied directly as style references. The result uses broad colour planes, simplified foliage and tapered cream highlights.

Photographic soil, labels, greenhouse structure and sky are deliberately omitted. True alpha allows the bright pale-sage strip backgrounds to show through, and allows small botanical overflows. The base strip areas remain 1100 × 330 and 1100 × 442 in the 1100 × 1430 cover coordinate system. The extended transparent assets are placed uniformly without stretching. The printed front remains 185 × 240 mm.

The separate reusable files are [courgette-illustration.png](courgette-illustration.png) and [tomato-illustration.png](tomato-illustration.png), exact copies of the generation outputs. The native [builder](build.py) retains the earlier centre lettering and sticker, with the sticker drawn last. A two-pixel inherited photographic seam was excluded from the blank top boundary; no lettering was redrawn. At 150 dpi the checked centre and author differ from Version 2 by at most 1/255 per channel, and the checked sticker interior is identical.

The full cover and a direct comparison with actual hero references have been visually inspected. There are no title/author collisions, obvious alpha halos or clipped overflows. These are focused concept checks, not print-production approval. The [16 November cream revision](../revision-27-version3-cream/README.md) supersedes this original front for current Version 3. Preserve these original assets. Do not rerun this historical builder into the current Version 3 paths, overwrite Versions 1/2, change the back or regenerate the main book.
