VEGETABLE GROWING / TROUBLES GUIDE

Brassica

Troubles

All listed crops: Broccoli · Brussels Sprouts · Cabbage · Cauliflower · Kale · Kohl Rabi

Check roots as well as leaves when brassicas wilt or stop growing. Mesh, sound transplants and steady growth prevent many losses; disease and nutrient problems need different responses. Test soil before liming or adding trace elements. Cabbage, cauliflower, sprouts and kohl rabi also have distinct quality problems.

Kohl Rabi

Woody Kohl Rabi

recognise

The swollen stem becomes hard and fibrous after drought, crowding, slow growth or late harvest.

act

There is no cure once woody. Use only parts that remain tender.

prevent

Grow steadily in fertile, moisture-retentive soil. Space correctly, water in dry spells and harvest young unless using a variety bred for large tender stems.

All listed crops

White Blister (White Rust)

recognise

Raised white blisters, mainly beneath leaves, may also affect stems or flowers and distort growth.

act

Remove affected leaves and reduce crowding.

prevent

Avoid repeating brassicas on the affected ground the following season.

All listed crops

Pigeons

recognise

Soft leaf tissue is stripped away, often leaving stalks and veins. Winter and early spring damage can be severe.

act

Cover vulnerable crops promptly; stripped leaves will not repair themselves.

prevent

Keep taut netting well clear of foliage and secure the edges. Check frequently for trapped wildlife.

All listed crops

Leaf Spot (Ring Spot)

recognise

Ringed leaf spots can yellow foliage and cause leaf fall; wet weather favours spread.

act

Remove badly affected leaves and improve airflow.

prevent

Rotate brassicas; avoid the affected site next season.
