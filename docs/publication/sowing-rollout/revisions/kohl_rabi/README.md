# Kohl Rabi · Quick facts, soil advice and varieties

6 November 2026. Pages 60–61, [approved by John](APPROVAL.json). [Imperial preview](REVIEW.html?unit=imperial) · [Metric preview](REVIEW.html?unit=metric).

The wider right column groups Sow, Row spacing and Plant spacing. It gives the early and late sowing qualifications and the extra space for giant varieties room to wrap. Ready in spans the left column below the four short facts. All eight values and their icons are retained, including the yield of 20 globes per 10 ft or 3 m row.

The first Soil & preparation instruction now reads “Choose a sunny site with fertile, well-drained soil.” The test-before-liming precaution remains in the club root row. This change is limited to the book proof.

The second row of Recommended varieties adds Green Vienna for spring or summer sowing, open-pollinated Lanro and Purple Vienna for a late crop. All three come from the [Kohl Rabi master catalogue](../../../../../../hackriculture-data/vegetables/kohl_rabi/kohl_rabi.json). UK catalogues list them by name. Rowel, the fourth reserve, has only general care advice in the master. The source priorities are preserved. [Thomas Etty’s 2026 catalogue](https://thomasetty.co.uk/seeds/vegetables-flowers-2026.pdf) · [Seed Co-operative’s kohlrabi list](https://www.seedcooperative.org.uk/collections/kohlrabi) · [Premier Seeds Direct’s Purple Vienna](https://premierseedsdirect.com/product/kohlrabi-purple-vienna-organic/).

All six varieties fit in two rows of three at the existing type size. In each edition, the clearance above the opening footer is now 10.36 mm, down from 33.49 mm. Page 61 retains 7.34 mm of clearance, two planting illustrations, eight pest rows and its See also link to Vegetable Troubles.
