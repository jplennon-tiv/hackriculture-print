Courgettes and marrows are the same crop at different stages: courgettes are picked young, firm and tender;
marrows grow larger for fresh use or storage. Modern courgette varieties keep producing small fruits, often
several each week in summer warmth. Total yield depends on season and picking.

Key risks
Slugs
Slugs chew seedlings,
especially in cold, wet
weather.
Mildew
White powder coats leaves,
often late in the season.
Poor pollination
If fruitlets yellow, wither or
fail to swell, pollination may
be poor.
Fruit rot
Fruit resting on wet soil may
soften and rot.
