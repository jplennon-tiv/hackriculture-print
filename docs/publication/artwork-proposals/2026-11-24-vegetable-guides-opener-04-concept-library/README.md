## Use and limits

These are generated photographic ideas, not documentary photographs or final print files. Different growth stages and new crop placements are intentional concepts grounded in the camera settings. New cropped scenes omit competing landmarks.

The contact sheets are for choosing subjects and compositions. Selected pictures should be redrawn individually from the original camera references and checked at full size for likeness, plants, hand actions, crop attachments and exact site details. Do not use enlarged contact-sheet tiles as print originals. Candidate 13 has more leaf damage than ideal and would need calmer, healthier foliage if shortlisted.
