24 November 2026. John said the olive two-column layout works well, but found the tomato too dominant as a single focal point. He requested a trial with **image 7**, the red cabbage with subtle caterpillar damage and no visible caterpillar.

This trial has quieter colours than the tomato, but the cabbage heart is still a focal point.

`SOURCE.json` records the contact sheet and exact panel extraction. `build.py` reuses the previous fonts/contents and exact accepted geometry. The new photograph crop is in `assets/`; earlier tomato and cabbage sources remain preserved. `MANIFEST.json` records the checks and hashes.

The rendered page was visually reviewed for image placement and text readability. This is a screen trial using the exact 421 × 528 px panel (about 54.52 ppi). Before any adopted version goes to the printer, produce/upscale the chosen artwork, inspect detail and verify at least 300 ppi at final placement.
