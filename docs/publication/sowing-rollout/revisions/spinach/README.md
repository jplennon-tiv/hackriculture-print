# Spinach one-off amendment

10 November 2026. [Review both editions](REVIEW.html). **Approved by John in both editions**, including the introduction clarification. The [approval record](APPROVAL.json) binds the reviewed files.

All four existing recommendations are true spinach. The small seasonal labels
now say **TRUE SPINACH · SUMMER** for Medania, Matador and Monterey F1, and
**TRUE SPINACH · WINTER** for Giant Winter. Their descriptions remain exact,
including Monterey’s additional suitability for protected winter growing.

A fifth entry features **New Zealand spinach**, labelled **SUMMER ALTERNATIVE**.
It is a separate crop, commonly offered by UK suppliers under that name. No
extra cultivar name or unsupported popularity ranking is invented. The copy:

> Heat-tolerant but frost-tender; a spreading summer alternative. Cook the tender shoots in water and discard the water.

Its characteristics and cooking instruction come from the canonical New Zealand
entry and soil advice. [Source and type decisions](../../../../../../hackriculture-resources/summaries/spinach-types/SOURCES.md)
record the exact fields, local-reference reading and UK supplier listings.
At John’s request, the introduction now explains that New Zealand spinach is
“usually sold simply as ‘New Zealand spinach’ without a variety name”. This
describes normal catalogue naming without claiming that named selections never
exist. It fits within the existing introduction height in both units, with all
other introduction wording and the distinction from perpetual spinach retained. Neither
canonical records nor their variety rankings are changed.

The panel retains the normal type sizes and uses two rows, three entries then
two. All nine Quick facts and icons remain; the final fit groups six shorter
facts on the left and Ready in, Sow and Plant spacing on the right. The scoped
fit pass does not reduce type. Soil & preparation loses only the routine pH
range and test-before-liming sentence. The manganese-deficiency diagnostic and
its specific precaution remain intact in the table.

Both actual PDFs retain two 185 × 240 mm pages, physical folios 84–85, all six
pest rows, original artwork and all three See also destinations. Footer
clearances are 5.05 mm on page 1 and 3.49 mm on page 2 in both units. No table
rows were removed. The full collection reader and main book remain unchanged.
