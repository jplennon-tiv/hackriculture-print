# Rhubarb one-off amendment

7 November 2026. [Review both editions](REVIEW.html). John approved both editions, including
the six-variety layout; the [approval record](APPROVAL.json) binds the exact reviewed files.

John chose to remove the sentence about crown divisions retaining a named
variety, finding the explanation self-evident. Plant crowns now ends:

> Choose a bought crown or divide a vigorous dormant plant. Set the piece upright with its buds just above the soil. Firm and water.

The practical planting steps and seed-growing advice remain unchanged. This
supersedes the earlier explanatory sentence. The existing Final tips wording
is outside this narrowly requested Sowing & planting edit and is retained.

The standing Quick facts check groups Harvest and Yield above Row spacing and
Plant spacing on the left; Plant crowns, Sow and Ready in share the right.
All seven facts retain their exact values and icons. The equal-width columns'
content heights are 31.58 mm and 30.54 mm in both units, a 1.04 mm difference.
Type sizes remain unchanged.

Soil & preparation loses the general sentence “Rhubarb tolerates slightly acid
soil; do not lime routinely.” It is not a definite crop-specific requirement.
The compost, drainage and root-space guidance remains; no liming instruction
is substituted. Canonical data is unchanged.

John then requested more varieties to use the opening page's spare space.
A third row adds **Fulton's Strawberry Surprise** and **Stockbridge Arrow**,
retaining the original four entries and their descriptions. Six varieties now
use three rows of two at unchanged type sizes and column widths. The extra row
uses 15.64 mm of the previous trailing space, leaving 9.81 mm above the footer.

The new descriptions are researched book-only additions. The master catalogue
has only one unused entry, Champagne Early, whose exact modern identity remains
uncertain in the previous review. It is preserved in the master and not silently
renamed as Champagne or Red Champagne. [Sources and claim record](../../../../../../hackriculture-resources/summaries/rhubarb-extra-varieties/SOURCES.md).
The two new entries use RHS and growers' descriptions; no master catalogue,
rank, existing variety wording or growing measurement changes. Stockbridge
Arrow's disputed season label is omitted; its red stems and forcing suitability
are supported. No shared rendering code changed: the scoped amendment retains
the existing two-column presentation and adds a third row.

Both actual PDFs retain two 185 × 240 mm pages, folios 80–81, six varieties,
four selected pest rows, original artwork and 9 pt planting text. Footer
clearances are 9.81 mm and 6.72 mm in both units. The previous Flowering table
omission remains: its practical instruction to remove flower stalks is already
in Looking after the crop. No additional pest rows are removed. This guide's
existing footer has no See also links, and that is unchanged.
