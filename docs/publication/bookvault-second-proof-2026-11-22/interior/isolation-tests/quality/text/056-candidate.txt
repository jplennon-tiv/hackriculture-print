Kale, or borecole, produces leaves and spring shoots instead of a heart. Varieties range from curled green leaves
to red or flat-leaved types. It stands through autumn and winter, and the leaves often taste sweeter after frost.

Recommended varieties
MODERN AND COLOURED
Nero di Toscana
A dark, strap-leaved kale for
repeated picking. Its textured
leaves are especially useful in
soups, stews and winter cooking.
CURLY-LEAVED
Reflex F1
A green curly kale for productive
winter picking. A useful maincrop
choice where familiar curled leaves
are preferred.
MODERN AND COLOURED
Red Russian
A flat-leaved kale with purple
stems and tender young leaves.
Useful for salads when young and
cooking when larger.
MODERN AND COLOURED
Redbor F1
A purple-red curly kale that adds colour to the plot.
Pick regularly for tender leaves and continued growth.
MODERN AND COLOURED
Yurok F1
A dark-leaved kale in the Tuscan style. Retain as a
modern alternative where this leaf shape is preferred.
