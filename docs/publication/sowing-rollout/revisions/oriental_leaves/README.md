# Oriental Leaves one-off amendment

7 November 2026. [Review both editions](REVIEW.html). John approved both editions;
the [approval record](APPROVAL.json) binds the exact reviewed files. Scope: the usual Quick facts and pH/liming checks only.

Quick facts previously used different groupings in imperial and metric, with
some long entries squeezed into narrow cells. Sow, Plant out and Harvest now
each use the full left-column width; Sowing depth, Row spacing, Plant spacing
and Ready in occupy the right column. The column widths are 48:52, with the
same order in both editions. All seven facts, exact values, qualifications,
icons and type sizes are retained. The agreed omission of Yield remains.

Soil & preparation loses only “Most of these brassica crops dislike very acid
ground.” This is general acidity advice rather than a definite treatment need,
so it is removed under John's standing crop-review rule. The compost, drainage,
moisture-retention and seedbed advice remains unchanged. Canonical gardening
data is unchanged.

Both actual PDFs remain two pages, 185 × 240 mm, numbered 70–71. Footer
clearances are 10.66 mm and 9.19 mm in both editions. All five varieties, eight
pest entries, original artwork, 9 pt planting text and See also links remain.
No pests are trimmed and no other wording changes. Both PDF spreads were
visually inspected against their original editions. The one-off reader's
links, images and unit selector work over the preview server and locally.
The original full readers and main book remain separate.
