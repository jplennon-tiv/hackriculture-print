Spinach can give quick leafy crops through much of the year, but needs rich soil and steady moisture to avoid
bolting. True spinach has summer and winter forms. New Zealand spinach is a separate, frost-tender summer
substitute for hot weather, usually sold simply as ‘New Zealand spinach’ without a variety name. Perpetual
spinach (spinach beet) is a leaf beet. Match type to season and pick tender leaves regularly.
