## Content and composition

The new material fills the lawn-clearance omission without rewriting the
existing two Digging pages. Three illustrated rows explain lifting manageable
turves, stacking them for later use, and the alternative of burial during
double-digging. Potting and returning finished material to soil are both included.

retained. A short peach panel covers persistent weed roots.
