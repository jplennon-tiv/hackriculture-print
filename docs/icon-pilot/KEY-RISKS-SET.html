Hackriculture · Key Risks only

The complete Key Risks drawing set.

15 drawings are approved: the original six and all nine Growth and Weather drawings. The remaining 20 need reworking. That gives 35 symbols: replacements for all 34 existing risk drawings, plus the distinct Small roots symbol. Each is shown beside its current counterpart, enlarged and at the existing 32px print size.

Pests and Disease and Damage were rejected as too child-like. See the revised pigeon study. Crop bubbles are deferred while their purpose is reconsidered. No production artwork, page mappings or gardening records have changed. The broad disease symbols work with their written labels; they are not diagnostic illustrations.

All masters are transparent, recolourable SVGs; PNG companions are 384 × 384 in the current dark ink. “Mildew” replaces the old powdery_mildew artwork and “Clubroot” replaces club_root. Those aliases do not change saved condition identities.

Mapping review remains separate: mouse will not stand for badger; poor germination will not stand for bitterness; forked roots will not stand for small roots. Additional meanings can be drawn after their assignments are agreed. No automatic keyword remapping is included.
