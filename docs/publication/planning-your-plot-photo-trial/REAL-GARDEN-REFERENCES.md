# Planning your plot: real garden references

John rejected the invented garden pair on 18 November 2026. He confirmed that his allotment uses finger paths. The following are visual research leads for the fixed artwork spaces on pages 154 and 155. External photographs and film frames have not been cleared for book publication.

| Layout | Photographed site | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finger paths | John's allotment, Cornwall. [A02 drone still](../../assets/garden-media-library/local-references/allotment/A02-0017-00135.000.jpg) | Use the pale green shed and John's boundary correction to identify his ground. This wide frame establishes ownership. For print, select a closer frame in which a path entrance, its blind end and the continuous growing ground beyond it are visible together. |
| Conventional paths | [Homeacres trial beds, Charles Dowding](https://charlesdowding.co.uk/blogs/homeacres/mid-may-17-sowings-plantings-videos-weeds-and-news), [three-strip photograph](https://uploads-ssl.webflow.com/644a89905644b6ece0c63cde/6507bf1003c64193d32381f7_Name_11.jpg) | The May 2017 view shows rectangular beds at everyday scale, with changes of crop, worn routes and covers. Dowding gives the bed width as 1.2 m and the paths as 40 cm. |
| Conventional paths, Cornish alternative | [Heligan Kitchen Garden, photograph by Ian Capper](https://www.geograph.org.uk/photo/5662825), [Historic England's account](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1000538?section=official-list-entry) | The vegetable garden has a central walk crossed by a transverse walk dividing it into quarters. Capper's frame gives local character and working crops. For teaching the layout, favour a viewpoint that shows the crossing itself. Geograph links the image's reuse terms. |
| Keyhole bed | [The wrap-around garden, Russ Grayson](https://medium.com/permaculture-3-0/the-wrap-around-garden-6bd55f76f0fd) | The opening Randwick photographs distinguish the finished example at the Sustainability Hub from a construction scene at the old Community Centre. The latter shows the brick edging, access path and room for gardeners to crouch. This is the walk-in working pocket described on page 154. |
| Keyhole bed, British alternative | [Pippa Chapman's garden at Esholt Hall, printed page 15, bottom photograph](https://people.wou.edu/~taylors/FYS207_WOU_Earth_Corps/35_Chapman_2023_Mandala_Community_Garden.pdf#page=2) | Willow woven around an actual woodchip-floored pocket; established herbs overlap its margins. A strong reference for how a working bed ages. The angle and foliage make the overall plan less clear than the Randwick construction example. |
| Leaf paths | [Wild Abundance, Sanford Way campus](https://www.wildabundance.net/about/our-permaculture-and-homestead-school-campus/), [overhead photograph](https://www.wildabundance.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Aerial-Permaculture-landscaping-at-Wild-Abundance-2025-1200-Current-.jpg.webp) | The drone view shows a crooked main route and angled subordinate routes among real crop rows. Inspect the upper half, where the branching is easiest to read. The whole plot is more complex than the book diagram. |
| Leaf paths, moving reference | [Sébastien Gourdin's garden in Combrand, film chapter at 6:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GMSsZKoTXPE&t=412s), [the grower's garden in 2020 and on the return visit](https://www.permaculturedesign.fr/potager-permaculture-5-ans-apres/) | The filmmakers identify the chapter as leaf-shaped paths and planting islands. In the earlier aerial photograph, irregular growing islands and paths are exposed. The later canopy hides more of the layout. |
