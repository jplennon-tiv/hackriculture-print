About the author

John Lennon has been growing food organically since 1989. With a background in software design and engineering, he brings a tinkerer’s curiosity to his garden and allotment in Cornwall. Through his Hackriculture videos, he shares practical growing methods and the lessons learned along the way, including a few ideas that worked rather better on paper.
