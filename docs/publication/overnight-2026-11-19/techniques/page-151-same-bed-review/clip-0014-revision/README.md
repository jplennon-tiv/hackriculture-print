The clipped evergreen overhangs a section of wall, and timber boards top the blockwork farther right. The [18:00 wall close-up](May-0014-18m00s-West-Wall.jpg) and [18:10 shed view](May-0014-18m10s-Sheds.jpg) confirm the materials and separate structures. The earlier angle at 02:18 cropped away this relationship.

Both pictures use the new [square crop](May-0014-10m31.6s-Square.png) as their location source. The [May broad bean image](Broad-Beans-May-0014.png) lifts the deeper shadows and adds three attached pods using the [11:30 footage](May-0014-11m30s-Pods.jpg) from the same row. The [December Cavolo Nero image](Cavolo-Nero-December-0014.png) reconstructs the follow-on crop in the bed under overcast light. It shifts the viewpoint a little and removes the preceding crop's temporary supports.
