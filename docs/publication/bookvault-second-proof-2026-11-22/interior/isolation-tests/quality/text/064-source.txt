Fancy making your own growing medium?
If you don’t want to buy a complete kit, oyster mushrooms will grow in clean straw from a friendly farmer
or bedding straw from a pet shop. You will need a suitable PVC box or strong plastic bag to keep the
medium contained and clean. A spawn supplier can recommend a suitable wrapping or container and may
be able to supply it. Buying commercial spawn is always recommended – you need to be certain what type
of mushrooms you are growing!
Prepare the straw
Put the straw in a heatproof container and pour
boiling water over it. Follow the supplier’s advice
on how much water to use and how long to soak it.
Let the straw cool to room temperature and drain
off the excess water.
Add the oyster spawn
With clean hands, layer the damp straw and spawn
in your chosen wrapping or container. Follow the
supplier’s advice on air holes. Once white threads
have spread through the straw, give the
mushrooms fresh air and indirect daylight.

Key risks
Mushroom flies
Small flies around the trays may be the
first sign. Their larvae feed in the
compost and can damage young
mushrooms.
Contaminating moulds
Contaminating fungi can discolour
mushroom substrate or distort and
decay developing mushrooms.
Poor cropping
Slow colonisation or stalled cropping
may result from unsuitable
temperatures, moisture or ventilation.
