How to use the guides

Reading the calendar

Bright: usual months for lowland England (US zones 8 to 9). Muted: options that may need suitable varieties, protection or milder conditions. Sowing may include indoor starts. For planting out, read Sowing & planting and check local conditions.

Difficulty rating

Difficulty reflects ease of growing and tolerance of adverse conditions. Radishes suit beginners; celery and cauliflower can defeat even experienced gardeners in a bad season.

Core needs

Too little sun, water or manure can damage some plants, while too much can harm others. Use the scales as a guide to each crop’s needs.
