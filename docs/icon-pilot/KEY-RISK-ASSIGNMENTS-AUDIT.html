Key Risks: assignment audit

44 crops · 173 displayed cards · 129 keep · 8 refine · 33 replace · 3 missing

24 September 2026. Every currently displayed risk checked against its crop-specific prose and the actual approved drawing. 30 crops have an icon concern; 25 have a replacement or missing-icon issue. Six crops also show duplicate identities. These are review findings: no assignments, artwork or gardening records have been changed.

“Keep” allows a useful general symbol for the right subject. “Refine” is related but weak. “Replace” points to the wrong subject, organ or process, or loses the meaning. Icon judgments are editorial; source links support the biological distinctions.

First fixes: chocolate spot → leaf spot; halo blight → trial leaf spot; Jerusalem-artichoke stem rot → trial stem rot; chicory soil caterpillars → caterpillar; mushroom flies → fly; beet-leaf-miner alias → fly after deduplication. All are proposals and need crop-aware mappings.

New-art priorities: allium basal rot, leafy-heart rot, storage-root texture/lesions, tomato splitting, cucurbit fruit/stalk problems, sweet-corn pollination, potato scab/nematodes, mushroom problems and the three rhubarb meanings. Bitterness and cross-pollination lack a dependable visible defect: use a crop/process cue with the label, not invented symptoms.

Selection issues: Club Root/Clubroot repeats on broccoli, Brussels sprouts, cabbage and cauliflower; beet leaf miner repeats under its Mangold Fly alias; parsnip repeats poor germination. Fix these before deciding the final artwork list, because another risk will become visible.

Parsnip’s Carrot Fly text also refers to “carrots”. The HTML scan emitted layout-budget warnings for broad bean, French bean and courgettes/marrows, plus the documented mushroom sowing fallback; no PDFs were generated or layout changes made.
