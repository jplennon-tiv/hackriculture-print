## Leave these alone for now

Globe Artichoke, Brussels Sprouts, Cauliflower, Celeriac and Sweet Corn can just fit one more short entry in the exploratory settings, but leave only **3.33–3.74 mm** above the footer. They are not strong candidates for filling a visible gap. Spinach has room within its present panel for one more short entry, but its recent five-entry version is already well filled.

The later expanded lists — Asparagus, Kohl Rabi, Parsnip, Rhubarb, Swede and Greenhouse Tomato — already make good use of the space. Retain Pea's explicitly chosen fuller introduction and six varieties. Preserve Mushroom's growing-medium panel. Salsify & Scorzonera has a real gap, but its [previous research](../sowing-rollout/revisions/salsify_scorzonera/README.md#additional-variety-10-november) explains why the apparent remaining names do not justify another distinct entry.

## Evidence and limits

- Used all **44 latest approved guides**, including the five sample crops and the later Garlic/Fennel yield and Pea-introduction revisions. The earlier full collection readers were not treated as current sources.
- Verified approval hashes for all **88 HTMLs, 88 PDFs and 88 spread images**. Source paths and record hashes are retained in [MEASUREMENTS.json](MEASUREMENTS.json).
- Inspected all **88 existing PDF opening-page images** in eight contact sheets; all 88 PDFs have two 185 × 240 mm pages and contain the expected selected variety names on page 1. [PDF checks](PDF-CHECKS.json). This was a focused opening-page audit, not a fresh review of every practical page.
- Remeasured all 88 approved openings after fonts and images loaded in print mode.
- Tested **41 temporary browser arrangements across 30 crops in both units**, using unchanged current entries and exact master short descriptions or first-sentence excerpts as measurement loads. [Capacity trials](CAPACITY-TRIALS.json). These excerpts are not finished new copy or horticultural recommendations.
- Tested four additional arrangements in both units: the three four-column proposals and a Leaf Beet six-entry capacity probe using duplicated existing entries as geometry loads only. [Alternative probes](ALTERNATIVE-TRIALS.json). Leaf Beet's 6.62 mm probe clearance does not establish which cultivars should be added.
- The raw reserve-name lists in the measurements are **not curated lists**. General crop types, aliases, shortened display names and uncertain identities must be filtered before recommending additions. In particular, Swiss Chard/Rainbow Chard are not automatically two extra named cultivars.
- No online variety-selection research was performed for this layout audit. Sources for any eventual additions should be checked at the drafting stage.
- No approved file, shared record, rendering code, full reader or book PDF changed. No app tests or book regeneration were needed. Changed-layout PDF proofs remain the next step if John requests amendments.
