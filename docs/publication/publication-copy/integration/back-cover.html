Growing vegetables raises plenty of questions: when to sow, how much space to leave, when to harvest, and what to do when something starts eating the crop before you do.

These illustrated guides bring the useful details together, with clear calendars, planting advice and organic ways to tackle common troubles.

There’s practical help with soil, compost, watering and greenhouses too. Use it to plan your plot, then turn to the guide you need as each job comes along.
