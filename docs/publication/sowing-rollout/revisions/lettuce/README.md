# Lettuce · Summer seed trays

6 November 2026. Pages 64–65, [approved by John](APPROVAL.json). [Imperial preview](REVIEW.html?unit=imperial) · [Metric preview](REVIEW.html?unit=metric).

The first Final tip reads “Keep summer seed trays cool and shaded.” It now uses the book’s [shade icon](../../../../../public/images/coloured-icons/style-a-v1/shade.svg), which shows an open-sided shade screen above a seed tray, with the sun outside. The other two Final tips keep their wording and artwork.

Sow and Plant out now share the wider right column with Row spacing and Plant spacing. All nine Quick facts retain their values and icons in both editions. The general acidity and lime directions have been removed. The reminder now advises deep watering for hearting lettuce, drawn from the master’s Looking after the crop. Soil & preparation keeps the advice to add compost to heavy clay.

Both editions have five varieties, nine pest entries, the original two planting drawings and the See also references. Clearance above the two footers is 14.30 mm and 6.21 mm.
