## First winter proof

[Earlier page proof](../../../../../../output/pdf/page-151-review/Vegetable-Guru-Page-151-Winter-Review.pdf) · [Page image](../../../../../../output/pdf/page-151-review/Vegetable-Guru-Page-151-Winter-Review.png).

The [May broad beans](Broad-Beans-Pods.png) are an edit of the same camera square from John's 25 May footage. The deeper shadows are lifted and three attached pods are visible. John's own photograph DSC08357 supplied the pod reference.

The [Cavolo Nero](Cavolo-Nero-Early-Winter.png) is a reconstruction of the same bed in early December. The mature plants have uneven crowns, scars from previous picking, drooping outer leaves and light incidental blemishes. The overcast light, damp soil, sparse neighbouring growth and fallen leaves draw on John's November allotment footage. The brick path, shed and water butt carry the viewpoint across the pair.

The field references show pronounced variation even within one row. Charles Dowding's [partly picked kale](https://charlesdowding.co.uk/blogs/homeacres/sept-15th-sow-salads-for-winter-no-dig-small-garden-harvests-watering) shows overlapping upright and hanging leaves. His [overwintering row](https://charlesdowding.co.uk/blogs/homeacres/february-2019-a-few-sowings-compost-and-mulching-courses-snow-and-pigeons) shows differences in plant lean and stems exposed by picking. The [Capchure photograph](https://www.homesandgardens.com/gardens/why-it-is-important-to-fertilize-a-vegetable-garden-in-the-fall) shows their leaf scars and irregular crown heights. See the [artwork record](ARTWORK.json) for exact photographs and the two prompts.
