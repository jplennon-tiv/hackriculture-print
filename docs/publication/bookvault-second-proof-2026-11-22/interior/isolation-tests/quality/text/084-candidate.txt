Recommended varieties
WINTER SQUASH
Crown Prince F1
Blue-grey winter squash with rich
orange flesh. Stores well for
roasting and winter meals.
WINTER SQUASH
Uchiki Kuri
Red-orange winter squash with
manageable fruits and rich flesh for
cooking and autumn storage.
WINTER SQUASH
Hunter F1
Pale-tan butternut with orange
flesh for autumn harvests and
winter meals.
WINTER SQUASH
Sweet Dumpling
Small, striped winter squash for individual portions,
alongside larger storage types.
PUMPKIN
Jack O'Lantern
Orange pumpkin mainly for Halloween carving. Give
trailing stems room.
Key risks
Slugs
Slugs attack seedlings and
young plants, especially in
cold wet conditions, and
may damage fruit resting on
the soil.
Mildew
Powdery mildew produces a
white coating on leaves,
often late in the season.
Stem damage and
rot
A broken stalk or damaged
skin provides an entry point
for storage rot.
No fruit
Poor pollination can leave
flowers without developing
fruit, especially in cool
weather.
