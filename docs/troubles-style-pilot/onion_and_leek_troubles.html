Troubles guide

Check alliums at the leaf base as well as above ground. Feeding damage, rot and growth checks can look alike. Use sound bulbs with thick necks or splits promptly; store only dry, healthy bulbs. White rot needs particular care to avoid spreading soil.

Onions and Shallots

Bolting

recognise

A premature flower stalk forms, often after cold or checked growth. Oversized sets can be more susceptible.

act

Pinch out small flower stems without loosening bulbs; use affected bulbs early.

prevent

Choose suitable small or heat-treated sets, plant at the recommended season and avoid growth checks.

Onions and Shallots

Saddleback

Bulbs split at base.

recognise

Bulbs split at the base, often after heavy rain or watering follows prolonged drought.

act

Use sound split bulbs promptly; they will not store well.

prevent

Keep growth steady with watering during dry summer spells.

Onions and Shallots

Set Division

recognise

Onion sets may form twin bulbs; large sets and growth checks can contribute. Shallots naturally form clusters.

prevent

Use suitable onion sets, plant in season and maintain steady growth. Shallot division is normal.

Onions and Shallots

Birds Pulling Sets

Newly planted onion sets are pulled out of the soil just as they begin to grow.

recognise

Newly planted onion sets are pulled out before they establish. Birds may investigate their exposed dry tips.

act

Replant sound lifted sets promptly before their roots dry out.

prevent

Firm sets at normal depth and guard with secure netting until rooted. Check for trapped wildlife.

Onions and Shallots

Onion Fly

Tunnelled bulbs with 1/4 in. white maggots.

recognise

Yellow, drooping leaves accompany maggots feeding at bulb bases. Seedlings may die; older plants fail to develop.

act

Remove badly affected plants and infested bulbs.

prevent

Use insect-proof mesh with rotation. Clear crop debris; sets are less vulnerable than seedlings.

Onions and Shallots

Drooping Leaves

recognise

Dark-green leaves may flop after excessive nitrogen. But drooping alone also needs checks for watering problems, pests or normal maturity.

act

Check roots and bulb bases before assuming a feeding problem.

prevent

Avoid fresh manure and excessive nitrogen; do not add fertiliser blindly.

Onions and Shallots / Leek / Garlic

Stem & Bulb Eelworm

Twisted, swollen leaves.

recognise

Swollen, distorted leaves accompany soft bulbs. Microscopic stem and bulb nematodes may kill young plants.

act

Remove affected plants and seek a confirmed diagnosis.

prevent

Avoid moving infested soil. Plan a suitable rotation for the identified nematode and its host range.

Leek

Smut

recognise

Black spots and blotches develop on leaves or bulbs of young plants; leaves thicken and twist. Leeks are particularly vulnerable.

act

Remove affected plants and seek confirmation.

prevent

Avoid moving affected soil and seek advice on a long crop break; the fungus can persist for years.
