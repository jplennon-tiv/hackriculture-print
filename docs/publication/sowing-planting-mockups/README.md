## What this trial shows

Each crop uses its existing opening followed by an establishment page and a
care/harvest/problems page. The first two pages face; the third is a verso on
the following spread. A three-thumbnail strip is not a physical spread.
Installing these would require a separate decision about neighbouring guides
and facing-page order. No extra blank leaves or new book pagination are proposed.

The establishment pages retain the full reviewed replacement prose at **9 pt**,
with distinct method/stage headings. Existing planting drawings remain. The
family colours, expressive opening hero/circle/difficulty sticker, category
labels, reminder banners, problem tables and Final tips treatment are carried
forward. Soil advice, harvest advice, pest rows, varieties, Key risks,
introductions and reminders retain their existing words. In particular, the
unapproved pH rewrites have not been introduced.

The initial narrow-panel trials used the original two-page arrangement and
planting beside the other advice, at several column widths. They overran the
footer even after moving the previously identified duplicate advice. These
measurements are in [CHECKS.json](CHECKS.json); they are evidence about those
arrangements, **not proof that every two-page redesign is impossible**. An
experimental table merger offered no saving and is absent from the final proofs.

The three-page versions are readable but intentionally expose the cost: roughly
50–85 mm of trailing space on establishment pages and 60–77 mm on final pages.
Carrot is especially uneconomical. This is a content/structure trial, not a
recommendation to add a page to every guide. The next discussion is whether to
retain full self-contained instructions, move shared explanations to Techniques,
or try a more substantial two-page composition. No further condensation or
roll-out has been authorised.

experiment. The metric proofs use the exact paired master dimensions, including
carrot 1.3 cm and leek 1.5 cm sowing depths. The proposed new clump spacings remain
12 in. / 30 cm for beetroot and 12–14 in. / 30–35 cm for leeks. Their evidence and

Only the already-identified duplications were removed from neighbouring copy:

- Carrot thinning moved from care into Sowing & planting.
- Beetroot care retains bird protection; clump/thinning instructions now live
  with their methods. Two duplicate Final tips moved into that explanation;
  the storage tip remains. Harvesting advice is unchanged.
- Leek hole-filling moved into the single-plant transplant method; routine
  watering, weeding and mulching remain in care.
- Short potato protection/earthing-up and tomato support reminders remain
  deliberately in ongoing care.

Quick facts names the operation and distinguishes single-plant spacing from
clumps. New timing facts use concise wording, with readiness and weather
conditions in the methods. Redundant “Between plants” is omitted where the
heading already says “Single plants”; all values and baby-leek qualifications
remain. Potato yield moved between Quick facts columns to balance the grid.
Beetroot, leek and tomato openings use slightly closer section gaps, with unchanged
text sizes. New action facts omit a repeated icon.

Calendar proposals are bounded: carrot says direct; beetroot distinguishes
direct sowing from early modules; leek adds a nursery-bed move-out band; outdoor
tomato distinguishes indoor sowing and final planting; potato says plant.
Beetroot has no invented planting-out month range. Month-wide bands are only
summaries; the precise dates, protection and frost qualifications remain in
Quick facts/prose. No shared calendar data or normal renderer changed.
The leek calendar's existing muted late-sowing months are carried forward;
their relationship to the ambiguous late-crop note remains an editorial review
point, not a newly resolved recommendation.
