**Status: research and proposed directions, not a selected redesign.** John has
opened the cover brief from a subtle makeover to a complete rework following

## Main finding

The useful distinction is the quality of composition and hierarchy, rather than
illustration versus photography. The selected references include restrained
illustration, a bright illustrated grid, a densely drawn garden, a harvest close-up
and a framed garden photograph. These are visual judgements about a small,
purposeful sample; no sales effectiveness or market-wide trend has been measured.

The production front combines several strong tile colours, separate crop motifs
and rounded display lettering. My assessment is that these compete for attention.
The back adds a large repeated title and several detailed page fragments. A clearer
order of attention and a more unified image could address the feedback before
requiring more realistic illustration. The original G2 has different modelling,
lettering and proportions from the physical proof, so they must not be treated as
the same design. Both originals and both production proposals remain untouched.

## Photography and channel coverage

The live [Hackriculture channel](https://www.youtube.com/@hackriculture) was
inspected for subject leads. This was a listing review, not a frame-by-frame search
of all videos. Six already-retained 480p stills were inspected alongside their
[September visual notes](../../../../hackriculture-resources/local/hackriculture-youtube/JyMOHsX0Hjs/VISUAL-NOTES.md)
and [drying beans/chillies visual notes](../../../../hackriculture-resources/local/hackriculture-youtube/tofhxOeYWPI/VISUAL-NOTES.md).
The review PDF links each image to its video and time.

None of those six scouting frames is selected as a finished front-cover image:
the spotted leek subject gives the wrong front-cover message; the beans in the
hand are soft; the wider views have competing structures, foliage or sky.
The greenhouse image with John may be useful author-context material, subject
to choosing a sharp original. Retained video/still caches were not modified.

Promising source leads for a later original-footage pass are:

- [Garden Plot in August](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S7J_rKUrZoA): crop colour and garden structure.
- [August At The Allotment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y1RCQXVBO00): established summer planting and potential harvest material.
- [Garden Tomato Surplus? Make Passata!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sIUbl3cKZsQ): possible harvest texture and abundance.

These leads are not quality-checked frames. Prefer an original photograph or sharp
high-resolution video frame; judge the actual portrait crop and space for type.
At 300 ppi, 185 × 240 mm is approximately 2,185 × 2,835 pixels before bleed.
A landscape 4K frame loses a substantial part of its width in a portrait crop;
pixel dimensions alone do not establish print suitability. An inset photographic
panel offers more flexibility than a full-cover photograph. No upscaling or
retouching has been performed.

## Proposed next comparison

Develop three equally considered front-cover trials, each at 185 × 240 mm and
using the existing title, subtitle and author wording initially:

1. **Refined existing idea:** cream/forest palette; calmer tile colour relationships,
   more coherent crop grouping and improved title spacing; compare a quiet grid
   with a single illustrated band. This is the conservative option.
2. **New illustrated composition:** one harvest grouping or growing scene, controlled
   texture and a more deliberate title area. This is the recommended first
   challenger because it offers a substantial change while retaining a direct
   connection to the illustrated interior.
3. **Authentic photograph:** one strong harvest or garden image from John's work,
   with a clean cream/deep-green title area. This is a credible alternative, dependent
   on a suitable original image.

Nunito Sans can connect supporting copy to the interior. Compare the existing
Lilita One title with a calmer display treatment rather than treating either a
serif or a muted palette as an automatic improvement. No replacement font has
been chosen or approved.

Compare the trials as online thumbnails, at actual panel size and beside an
approved interior spread. Assess title legibility, focal point, maturity, warmth,
subject clarity and the promise of a practical reference book. Take the chosen
system onto the back with a calmer blurb and one or two useful excerpts, using
the latest approved crop proofs if new fragments are needed. Competitor motifs,
layouts and lettering are references for principles, not templates to copy.

John's decisions still needed: preferred visual route, image subject, tolerance
for a new title treatment, and how prominent the interior excerpts should be on
the back. New introduction and cover wording remain a separate pending discussion.
