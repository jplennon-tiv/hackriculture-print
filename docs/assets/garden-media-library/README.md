## What the pictures cover

Salad leaves can be followed through sowing, watering and harvest (VG01, VG13, VG07). Peas are recorded on the plant and in the bowl (VG05, VG16). The onion photographs show the open mesh used for drying and the strings used for storage (VG11–VG12).

Close details of seed placement, emergence and thinning are gaps in this **selection**. The [channel catalogue](../../../../hackriculture-resources/local/hackriculture-youtube/CHANNEL-CATALOGUE.md) points to further shoots, including *February Seed Planting* and *April In The Greenhouse*. Use the published material as a lead and confirm the actual shot and its raw time before adding a camera-frame entry.

## Maintaining the register

Keep the VG identifiers stable. Add one row per useful composition, with crop, action, media type, availability and native dimensions. For a frame, give the source video, numeric seek and timebase. For an offline original, keep the original path and label the available preview and its dimensions separately. Date any later source check. For a derivative, retain its parent where known and the source record explaining the treatment.

The [contact-sheet builder](build-contact-sheet.py) uses the CSV's sheet-page column and working image paths. Run it with Python, Pillow, ReportLab and pypdf on the Mac. Photographs on the sheet are read from their original locations. The PDF includes its own JPEG previews for offline viewing. The current layout represents six images per page. The three small working [frame previews](frame-previews/) can be recovered from the recorded raw camera seeks.
