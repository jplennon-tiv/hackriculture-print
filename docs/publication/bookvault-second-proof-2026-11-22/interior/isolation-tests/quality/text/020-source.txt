Runner beans make a colourful screen as well as a crop. Red, white or pink flowers give way to long flat pods.
The plants need more moisture than French beans, especially when the pods set. Pick young pods every few
days. Older stringed varieties need a little more preparation in the kitchen.

Key risks
No pods
Runner beans often fail to
set pods when roots become
dry, when weather is very
hot and dry, or when
pollinating insects are
scarce.
Halo blight
Semi-transparent or water￾soaked spots with yellow
halos on leaves are a
warning sign.
Black bean aphid
Black aphids can gather on
soft runner-bean growth,
leaving sticky honeydew and
distorted leaves.
Slugs
Slugs are the chief pest of
young runner bean plants,
especially in cool, wet
weather while growth is
slow.
