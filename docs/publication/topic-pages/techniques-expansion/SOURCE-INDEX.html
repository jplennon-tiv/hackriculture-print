## Reconciliation, boundaries and omissions

- Compost turning: generic seasonal draft text says turn regularly; John's explicit myth-busting section and later detailed bin demonstration describe no routine turning. Use that supported authored method with a moisture/mix check when material stalls; do not reproduce the generic fragment.
- Two versus three bins: the 2026 video explicitly explains changing from two to three, so this is a dated development, not an unresolved conflict. Use three as preferred, two as a smaller-space compromise. Moisture: water dry additions in enclosed brick bins; check exposed/small bins in dry weather (later tips). No universal daily watering rule.
- Greenhouse soil replacement intervals conflict even within the manuscript (two years versus before tomatoes) and later no-dig experiments develop it further. Do not prescribe a frequency or move diseased soil around. Canonical tomato advice controls disease response: rotate where practical or use fresh container compost. Preserve this question for John if a future detailed chapter needs it.
- Compost inputs: no pet faeces, mixed cooked-food or manure-safety instructions in this beginner overview. The manuscript's pet-waste starter advice raises a substantive hygiene issue; it is withheld, not silently endorsed or replaced. The later compost exclusions video itself recommends council food-waste collection for cooked/fatty food where bin exclusion is inadequate. Use that conservative beginner scope. No guaranteed rodent-proof DIY claim or unspecified mesh dimensions.
- No generic thick raw straw mulch: retain John's wet-climate experience and mature-compost preference. No claims that organic food cures disease, speculative peat-ban history, industrial/copyright polemic, impossible seed-killing temperature, automatic nitrogen benefit from legumes, or unverified IBC/oil-tank plumbing modifications. All outside these overview briefs.
- Sowing broadcast: no mixed-depth blanket recommendation, pest-confusion guarantee, beach sand or unidentifiable thinnings as food. Optional compatible leaf mix, thin for its crops and follow their depths; shared JSON controls named crops.
- Installation is a practical overview, not an invented engineering specification: measure the manufacturer's exact base plan, level/square/anchor it, assemble/brace before glazing. No footing recipe from unfinished contradictory 1:5/5:1 notes; no alteration of a kit doorway as a casual instruction.
- No frozen cultivation content or full-book pagination changed. New proof order/folios are provisional; page 1 is an opening candidate, pages 2-11 techniques candidates. Sequential review pages do not imply final facing pairs.
