The saved first proof used Cover Board and matt lamination. No 300/350 gsm paperback upgrade is listed. The public quote-tool page and [Cover Board description](https://help.bookvault.app/cover-board-vs-self-cover) are consistent with this.

The documented [lamination choices](https://help.bookvault.app/what-laminations-do-you-offer) are matt and gloss. There is no documented heavy-duty/anti-scuff upgrade in that list. Switching to gloss changes the finish; there is no evidence here that it would solve John's concern about cover stiffness.

[Case Bound PPC](https://help.bookvault.app/what-binding-methods-do-you-offer) uses rigid boards with printed artwork and is Bookvault's materially sturdier alternative. Its [quote guide](https://help.bookvault.app/bookvault-case-bound-quote-guide) supports custom dimensions, but an exact 185 × 240 mm / 174-page / 150 gsm coated hardback quote and template have **not** been obtained. Changing to it is a binding decision, not a paperback-stock upgrade.
