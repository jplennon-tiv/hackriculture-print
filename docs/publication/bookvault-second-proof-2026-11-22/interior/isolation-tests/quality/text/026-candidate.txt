Calabrese produces a green central head in summer or autumn, followed by smaller side shoots. Sprouting
broccoli stands through winter and gives tender purple or white spears when other greens are scarce. Choose
the type for the season you want to harvest.

Recommended varieties
PURPLE SPROUTING
Early Purple Sprouting
An overwintering broccoli producing purple shoots in
spring. Allow it a long growing season and pick the
shoots regularly.
CALABRESE
Green Magic F1
An early calabrese producing a dense central head
followed by side shoots. A practical choice for a
summer broccoli crop.
PURPLE SPROUTING
Claret F1
A purple-sprouting broccoli for a later spring harvest.
Useful for extending cropping beyond earlier sprouting
varieties.
CALABRESE
Marathon F1
A dependable summer calabrese with firm blue-green
heads. A useful alternative to Green Magic for the
main crop.
