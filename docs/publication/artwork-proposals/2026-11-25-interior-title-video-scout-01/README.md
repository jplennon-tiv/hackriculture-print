## Search and selection

Reviewed the eleven existing summer scouting sheets and selected spring, early-summer and drone scouting sheets. Then decoded 89 additional moments across 29 source clips, including deeper sampling of working scenes and May footage. The selection covers the home garden and allotment, greenhouse harvesting, watering, raking, support-building, potato lifting, spring planting and summer crop views. Other people’s gardens and the kitchen footage were not shortlisted.

`SELECTION.json` records exact source paths, timecodes, setting, actor presence and editorial notes. `extraction-input.json` and `extraction-output.json` record native-frame extraction. `CHECKS.json` records source dimensions and image hashes. Original footage on mounted volumes was read only.
