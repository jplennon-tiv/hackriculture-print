# Vegetable Guides — photographic opener ideas

**24 November 2026 · Separate proposal · Awaiting John's selection**

Review **Vegetable-Guides-Contact-Sheet-02-Page-Proportions.png**. It offers six directions for the first main section opener (current folio 7):

1. The productive home garden.
2. Summer at the allotment.
3. A modest basket harvest.
4. From plot to kitchen — harvest on timber.
5. John with a handful of harvest.
6. John among the leaves.

These are generated photographic concepts grounded in the actual site and likeness references. They are not documentary photographs or print-ready artwork. Summer development of the garden references and the harvest arrangements are deliberate proposals.

The second sheet corrects the first sheet's overly narrow panels. Its panels approximate the 185 × 240 mm page proportions; exact print dimensions will be set when the selected image is redrawn. The photographs contain no page titles or contents. Numbering is outside the photographs.

Options 3 and 4 currently offer the easiest title backgrounds; options 1 and 2 emphasise place. Option 5 needs more headroom for a large title at the next stage. The chosen scene will need a full-size redraw from original camera sources, detailed likeness/plant checks, exact crop/bleed and a separate typography trial.

The existing contents list remains undecided: retain, remove, or test a bottom strip later. No book integration or supplier changes are authorised by this proposal.
