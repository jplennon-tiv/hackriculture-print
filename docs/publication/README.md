## YouTube Shopping compatibility — checked 6 October 2026

**Launch decision, 7 October:** John chose direct Bookshop links and deferred
Shopify to a later iteration. The comparison below remains research evidence;
it does not authorise creating or connecting a Shopify store.

**Finding:** Bookvault remains a viable printer/fulfilment service. Its own
retail stores are not listed as supported YouTube Shopping connections, and no
published author-level direct integration was found. Shopify and Wix appear on
both the [YouTube supported-platform list](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12258186?hl=en)
and [Bookvault integration list](https://help.bookvault.app/what-websites-do-you-have-apps-and-plugins-for).
That supports a connected own-store route, subject to channel/product approval;
it is not evidence of a tested hackriculture integration.

| Route | Viewer journey | Practical implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Great British Bookshop with ordinary links | Long-video description/pinned comment or channel link → individual book page → checkout | Simplest first-edition route; retail administration remains with the shop, but no verified native YouTube product tags/shelf |
| Own Shopify store + Bookvault app | YouTube product card → Hackriculture-branded store checkout → automated Bookvault fulfilment | Supported integration path; recurring shop/payment costs and John operates the retail business |
| Own Wix store + Bookvault integration | YouTube product card → Wix store checkout → Bookvault fulfilment | Also supported; subscription/payment costs need a separate quote if selected |

[Shopify's YouTube guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/marketplaces/google/youtube-shopping)
and [Wix's connection guide](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-connecting-your-youtube-channel-to-wix)
describe supported connections. For UK merchants, plan checkout on the store;
do not promise YouTube-native payment. Channel eligibility, Google Merchant
Center and product approval still need checking. YouTube Studio → Earn → Shopping
is the next channel-specific check if native Shopping is wanted; it was not
accessed during this research. The public guides differ in their subscriber
wording, so do not infer eligibility from subscriber count alone. The
[Google/YouTube channel requirements](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/google/requirements)
also require book ISBN product data and public store/shipping/policy information.
No ISBN has been allocated or rights declaration made.

Bookvault also documents [Buy Now links](https://help.bookvault.app/how-to-create-buy-now-links-in-bookvault)
