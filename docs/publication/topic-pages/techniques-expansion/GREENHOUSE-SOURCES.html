## John's approach, recorded before drafting

The full greenhouse chapter in `local/john-hackriculture-draft/hackriculture-draft.txt` (lines 2707–3132, including construction details and captions) and its source notes were read. The larger greenhouse uses a brick perimeter base and internal brick beds over soil. The extra rooting depth and moisture reserve reduce reliance on pots; the raised frame preserves headroom. Loosen soil compacted by construction before filling beds with topsoil and mature compost. Either dig compost in or maintain a no-dig surface dressing. Keep a clear central path.

The smaller greenhouse is a separate, simpler arrangement: a low outer base supports the frame, but there are no internal bed walls. Compost goes on the earth borders around a woodchip path. Its description as an experiment in the manuscript develops into an established arrangement in the later videos. Do not repeat the manuscript's blanket dismissal of floor-level growing: John explicitly requests this method and his later practice supplies the context. Present all three choices without ranking them.

Seasonal cropping follows a physical sequence: early salads or crops use spare space; remove them when summer plants need room; autumn salads follow cleared summer crops. This must be separate from the daily jobs of watering and ventilation, and winter cleaning/repairs. Avoid turning the overview into a complete growing calendar.

## Qualifications and deliberate omissions

- Floor-level no-dig is for usable, draining soil, not compost spread over paving. Keep the path and growing areas distinct and refresh the compost at the surface. Do not prescribe a new generic depth.
- The manuscript contains differing soil-replacement intervals. Do not choose either, or substitute the RHS interval. The initial disease-led paragraph used the canonical rotate/container alternative. John has now supplied a replacement adding fresh garden soil and compost for raised beds. Apply his direct wording in this draft; no routine interval is prescribed and canonical records are unchanged.
- Omit precise brick footings/mortar recipes and the manuscript's inconsistent mix ratios. These pages explain the growing arrangements, not structural masonry design.
- Retain glazing safety and model-specific base preparation. John's new assembly paragraph is reproduced as supplied.
- No first-person anecdotes in the page copy: John's latest impersonal rule governs this collection.
