Greenhouse cucumbers can produce fruit almost daily in hot weather. They like steady warmth and moist air, but
cold, wet compost damages their roots. Train the brittle stems up strings or canes. Check the packet before
removing male flowers.

Key risks
Red spider mite
Speckled, yellowing leaves
and fine webbing
underneath can indicate red
spider mite.
Bitterness
Stress, including uneven
moisture or temperature, can
increase bitterness.
Mildew
Powdery mildew often
appears on older leaves
later in the season.
Lack of warmth
Cold sowing or planting
conditions check growth and
can give slugs, woodlice and
soil diseases a chance to
damage young plants.
